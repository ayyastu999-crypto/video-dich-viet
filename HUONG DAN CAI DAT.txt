================================================================
  VIDEO DICH VIET
  Dich video tieng nuoc ngoai sang tieng Viet:
  nghe loi thoai -> dich -> long tieng -> chay phu de vao video
================================================================


CAN CHUAN BI TRUOC
------------------

1) Python 3.11 tro len
   Tai tai: https://www.python.org/downloads/
   QUAN TRONG: khi cai nho tick o "Add Python to PATH"

2) FFmpeg
   Mo PowerShell go:  winget install Gyan.FFmpeg
   Cai xong phai MO LAI cua so dong lenh thi may moi nhan.

3) (Khong bat buoc) Card do hoa NVIDIA
   Co GPU thi may nghe loi thoai nhanh hon nhieu lan, va dung duoc
   hai tinh nang: tach nhac nen, xoa phu de cu chay san tren video.


CAI DAT
-------

Buoc 1. Giai nen thu muc nay ra o dia bat ky.
        Nen dat o duong dan KHONG DAU tieng Viet, vi du:  D:\video-dich-viet

Buoc 2. Bam dup file:   Cai Dat.bat
        Cho khoang 5-15 phut. Neu may co GPU, no se hoi co cai
        phan GPU khong - go "c" roi Enter (tai them khoang 3GB).

Buoc 3. Bam dup file:   Dich Video Viet.bat
        Trinh duyet tu mo. Neu khong, vao: http://localhost:5177


LAN DAU DUNG: NHAP API KEY
--------------------------

App can 1 key de dich phu de. Phan nghe loi thoai chay tren may
nen khong ton tien.

Bam bieu tuong banh rang (goc tren ben phai) roi dan key vao.

  Gemini   - mien phi, lay tai https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
             Ban mien phi gioi han 20 luot goi moi ngay (~4 video).

  DeepSeek - tra phi nhung rat re (khoang 35 dong / video),
             khong gioi han luot. Lay tai https://platform.deepseek.com

Chi can MOT trong hai la chay duoc. Key luu tren may ban, khong gui di dau.


DUNG THE NAO
------------

Keo tha video vao khung, hoac dan duong dan file. Bam BAT DAU XU LY.
Xong se co:

  - Video da chay phu de tieng Viet va long tieng
  - File phu de .srt roi
  - Hai file kich ban .txt (van xuoi + song ngu) de copy di viet lai noi dung

Xem duoc video ket qua ngay tren trang, khong can tai ve.


MEO
---

- Video nguon co san phu de tieng Trung chay tren hinh?
  Mo file config/default.yaml, doi  blur_old_captions: false  thanh  true
  De phu de tieng Viet khong de chong len phu de cu.
  (Can da cai phan GPU o buoc 2)

- Chi muon phu de, khong can long tieng?
  Trong o "Giong doc tieng Viet" chon "Khong tao giong".
  Chay nhanh hon nhieu.

- Video ra file nang qua?
  Mo config/default.yaml, tang so  crf  len (24 -> 26 hoac 28).
  So cang lon file cang nhe.
